Neural Networks (STGNNs) [44, 45] and Transformers [10, 65],
can fully mine semantics from MTS, but they generally require
complete observations to maintain accurate forecasting [66]. In
reality, due to factors such as natural disasters and component
failures, data collectors can easily malfunction and fail to output
data normally [73]. In this case, existing models only use incomplete
observations to predict future values, limiting their performance
[12]. To illustrate, we evaluate the performance of several models
[43, 57] under different missing rates on the PEMS04 dataset. As
shown in Figure 1(a), the forecasting errors (Mean Absolute Error)
of these models increase significantly as the missing rate increases.
